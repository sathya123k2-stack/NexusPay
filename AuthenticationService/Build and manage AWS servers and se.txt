Kubernetes — Orchestration

Kubernetes is used when you have many containers/services and need something to manage them automatically.

For example:

Kubernetes
│
├── CustomerService container
├── AccountService container
├── TransactionService container
│
├── Restart failed containers
├── Scale containers
├── Load balance traffic
└── Manage deployments
